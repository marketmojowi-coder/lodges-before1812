Privacy policy

Lodges Before 1812 is published by Market Mojo LLC. This policy covers the app and the website at lodges.mktmojox.com.

What we collect. Your name and email address (from your Google account or the address you sign in with), your membership status, and any facts you add to the fact bins. Nothing else: no location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or tracking.

Why. To sign you in, to check your membership, and to show your facts to the editors who review them.

Payments. Memberships are paid on our website through Stripe. Card details go to Stripe and never reach us or the app.

Where it is kept. Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and Cloud Storage), encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Deleting your account. Open Delete my account in the app or on the website. Your sign-in, membership record and pending facts are deleted; accepted facts stay in the data set without your name.

Contact. Market Mojo LLC, through the contact address on www.mktmojox.com.
