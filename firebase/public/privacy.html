Privacy policy

Lodges Before 1812 is published by Market Mojo LLC. This policy covers the app and the website at lodges.mktmojox.com.

What we collect. Your name and email address (from your Google account or the address you sign in with), your membership status, and any facts you add to the fact bins. Nothing else: no location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or tracking.

Why. To sign you in, to check your membership, and to show your facts to the editors who review them.

Payments. Memberships are paid on our website through Stripe. Card details go to Stripe and never reach us or the app.

Where it is kept. Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and Cloud Storage), encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell your information and do not share it for advertising. The only services that handle it for us are Google Firebase (sign-in, database and hosting) and Stripe (payments), each under its own data protection terms.

How long we keep it. While your account is open. When you delete your account, your sign-in, membership record and pending facts are removed at once, and anything left in backups is gone within 30 days. Stripe keeps payment records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights. Under My account you can see what we hold, download it (Download my data), withdraw pending facts, manage or cancel your membership, and delete your account. Wherever you live, including California (CCPA/CPRA) and the European Union or United Kingdom (GDPR), you may also ask us to correct or delete your information or explain how it is used; we answer within 30 days. We rely on our contract with you (your membership) and your consent (the facts you choose to submit) to use it.

Deleting your account. Open Delete my account in the app or on the website. Your sign-in, membership record and pending facts are deleted; accepted facts stay in the data set without your name.

Children. The app is for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children; if you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and members are told by email of any change to what we collect.

Contact. Market Mojo LLC, through the contact address on www.mktmojox.com.
