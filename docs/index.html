Lodges Before 1812

A proprietary research data set of the Masonic lodges of George-Town and the City of Washington before 1812, from the book series by Thomas M. Saharsky.

What members get

  • 4,764 records across seven lodges, the Royal Arch Chapter and Washington Parish, 1789-1811
  • Search by man, lodge, year, trade and building
  • Every record cited to its book and to where the original is held: the Grand Lodge of Maryland returns, National Archives Record Groups 42 and 45, the Library of Congress, newspapers and parish archives
  • Fact bins: new facts with their sources, reviewed before they count
