Civic Atlas

Potomac Territory 1700–1811

A proprietary research data set of the Masonic lodges of George-Town and the City of Washington before 1812, from the book series by Thomas M. Saharsky.

What members get

  • Nearly 5,000 records across seven lodges, the Royal Arch Chapter and Washington Parish, 1789–1811
  • Search by man, lodge, year, trade and building
  • Every record cited to its book and to where the original is held, down to the record group, series, volume and box: the National Archives (Record Groups 42 and 45), the Library of Congress, the Stephen J. Ponzillo Jr. Memorial Library and Museum, Christ Church, Washington Parish, and others. See Sources.
  • Works offline in the Android app after the first sign-in
  • Fact bins: new facts with their sources, reviewed before they count
