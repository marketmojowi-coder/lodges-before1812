Historical records

It is compiled with care from the sources named in each record, but historical records are incomplete and sometimes contradictory. Readings, dates, spellings, identifications of men and the positions of sites can be wrong. Where the records do not state a point plainly, the app says so and holds it back from the counts. Before relying on any record, consult the original at its holder, named in the record and on the sources page.

Maps

Map positions are approximate, drawn from the street corners and lots named in the records and set on modern streets. The 1800 shoreline is approximate.
